I'm a bioinformatics scientist at Washington University School of Medicine. I study clonal hematopoiesis across nearly 1 million participants in UK Biobank and All of Us, working with clinicians to understand who develops it and how it progresses to blood cancer. I build the pipelines and prediction models behind this work. During my PhD at the University of Nevada, Reno, I developed deep-learning methods for single-cell and cancer multi-omics data.

### Papers and projects
- **Plasma proteomic signature predicts myeloid neoplasm risk** (Clinical Cancer Research, 2024): I used UK Biobank plasma proteomics to show that circulating protein levels help predict which people with clonal hematopoiesis go on to develop myeloid cancers, and used genetic scores to separate likely causes from downstream markers. [paper](https://doi.org/10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-23-3468) · [code](https://github.com/kbolton-lab/CCR_Proteomics)
- **Predictive modeling of clonal hematopoiesis across diverse cohorts** (Blood Advances, 2026): Models that estimate who is likely to carry clonal hematopoiesis from routine blood counts and clinical data, released as a Dockerized web app so others can score their own cohorts. [paper](https://doi.org/10.1182/bloodadvances.2025017940) · [prediction app](https://github.com/kbolton-lab/ch-prediction)
- **Germline genetic variation impacts clonal hematopoiesis landscape and progression to malignancy** (Nature Genetics, 2025): A multi-cohort study of how inherited variation shapes which clonal hematopoiesis mutations people acquire and whether they progress to blood cancer. [paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-025-02250-x) · [code](https://github.com/kbolton-lab/germlineCH_NG)
- **ArCH** (Bioinformatics, 2024): A cloud-based WDL pipeline that combines several variant callers with artifact filtering to detect low-frequency clonal hematopoiesis mutations in large sequencing studies. [paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btae121) · [code](https://github.com/kbolton-lab/ArCH)
- **ch-toolkit**: A Python toolkit used alongside ArCH to annotate, filter, and manage clonal hematopoiesis variant calls at population scale. [code](https://github.com/kbolton-lab/ch-toolkit)
